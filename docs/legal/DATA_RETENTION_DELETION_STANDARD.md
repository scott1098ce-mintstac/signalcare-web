# Data Retention & Deletion Standard

**Version:** 2026-09-07.1  
**Status:** Interim governance for launch (no automated deletion)

## Clinical monitoring records

Retain for a **7-year baseline** after the last relevant monitoring activity, unless a longer applicable requirement applies due to:

- jurisdiction
- patient age
- professional obligation
- contract
- legal hold
- other applicable requirement

## Other classes

- Staff/auth records: retain while account/membership active and as needed for security/audit
- Invitations and audit events: retain for security and accountability
- Provider logs/backups: subject to provider retention (confirm externally)

## Deletion / archive

- Soft archive removes patients from active directory views; underlying clinical history remains
- Hard deletion is not a product feature at launch
- Deletion/erasure requests are handled operationally under the Privacy Access & Correction Procedure
- Do not destroy records under legal hold

## Prohibition

No destructive bulk production deletion as part of routine launch operations.
