# Privacy Access & Correction Procedure

**Version:** 2026-09-07.1  
**Status:** Operational (manual process for launch)

## Channels

- Privacy email: **hello@signalcare.io**
- Clinic staff may also receive patient requests directly

## Process

1. Log the request date, requester identity, clinic relationship, and scope.
2. Verify authority (patient, authorised representative, or clinic acting for its patient relationship).
3. Locate records in Sydney production systems (patients, enrolments, messages, alerts, notes, audits). Do not invent missing provenance.
4. Provide access or correction via clinic-mediated process or SignalCare operational export as appropriate.
5. Record completion and any refusal grounds.
6. Escalate legal/edge cases to founder.

## Product capability at launch

- No patient portal
- No automated SAR pack
- Soft archive exists; hard delete is not a product API
- Staff can update patient identity fields in-product
