# Founder Legal Risk Memorandum (Internal)

**Version:** 2026-09-07.1  
**Audience:** Founder only (not public)  
**Status:** Launch under founder-prepared framework; external lawyer review deferred until commercially justified

## Position

SignalCare is launching initial commercial clinic use under a founder-approved Australian privacy/legal pack derived from Phase 6C architecture facts and Phase 6E implementation.

## Known residual risks (non-exhaustive)

1. Corporate entity / ABN / registered address not yet finalised in public documents (trading as SignalCare; contact hello@signalcare.io).
2. Twilio and other providers may process SMS content outside Australia; wording must remain truthful and not over-claim residency.
3. Soft archive only; erasure/portability are operational, not automated product features.
4. OpenAI capability exists in secrets but is not injected into ECS; must remain off unless separately approved.
5. External legal review is deferred deliberately; this increases residual legal uncertainty relative to counsel-reviewed documents.
6. Test Aesthetics may lack Terms acceptance until founder accepts current Terms for that organisation.

## Deliberate non-goals at launch

Patient portal, automated deletion, cookie CMP, enterprise DPA negotiation workflows, enabling media/clinician PHI email/external AI, ISO/HIPAA theatre.

## Decision

Proceed to first real patient only after Phase 6F closure audit confirms production controls match this pack.
