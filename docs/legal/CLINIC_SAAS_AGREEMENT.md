## 1. Agreement

These terms form the SignalCare Clinic SaaS Agreement between SignalCare and the clinic customer that uses the SignalCare service ("Clinic").

This Agreement is version 2026-09-07.1 and is effective from 7 September 2026, unless an Order Form states a different start date for a particular engagement.

If an Order Form applies, commercial particulars in the Order Form (such as fees, plan, term, and included clinics) control those commercial topics. These terms control the remaining service, privacy, and risk allocation topics.

## 2. The SignalCare service

SignalCare provides software and related services that support post-procedure patient monitoring and clinic workflow, including SMS check-ins, rules-based monitoring outputs, alerts, reviews, and related operational tools.

SignalCare provides monitoring and workflow support. SignalCare does not practise medicine and does not provide clinical diagnosis, treatment, or emergency response.

## 3. Clinical responsibility

The Clinic remains solely responsible for diagnosis, treatment, clinical decisions, emergency response, clinical follow-up, and acting on monitoring information.

SignalCare outputs are decision-support tools for the Clinic. They do not replace clinician judgement or the Clinic duty of care.

Patients with urgent or severe symptoms require appropriate urgent medical care. SignalCare is not an emergency service.

## 4. Clinic responsibilities

The Clinic must use SignalCare only for legitimate clinical and operational purposes for patients under its care or authority.

The Clinic decides whom to enrol, enters patient details accurately, configures and operates monitoring appropriately, and ensures staff use accounts in accordance with Clinic policies and this Agreement.

The Clinic is responsible for training its users and for reviewing alerts, reviews, and other monitoring outputs in a clinically appropriate timeframe.

## 5. Patient authority and consent

Before enrolling a patient in SignalCare monitoring, the Clinic must ensure it has the authority and patient agreement required for that monitoring, including SMS communications where used.

At enrolment, Clinic staff must confirm that the patient has agreed to participate in SignalCare monitoring and has been provided with, or given access to, the current SignalCare Patient Privacy and Monitoring Notice.

Clinic confirmation in SignalCare records monitoring participation and notice provision. It is not clinical treatment consent and does not replace any separate treatment consent the Clinic requires.

## 6. Accounts and security

The Clinic must keep account credentials confidential, assign roles appropriately, and promptly disable access when a staff member no longer needs it.

The Clinic must notify SignalCare promptly of suspected unauthorised access to Clinic accounts or of security incidents affecting SignalCare use.

SignalCare may suspend or limit access where reasonably necessary to protect the service, other customers, or personal information.

## 7. Privacy and data handling

Each party must handle personal information in accordance with applicable Australian privacy law and this Agreement.

The SignalCare Privacy Policy describes how SignalCare handles personal information in connection with the service. The Patient Privacy and Monitoring Notice is the short patient-facing notice used at monitoring commencement.

For patient monitoring data, SignalCare processes personal information on the Clinic instructions to provide the service. The Clinic remains responsible for the lawfulness of its instructions and for patient-facing notices and consents it is required to provide.

## 8. Data Processing schedule summary

Subject matter: operation of SignalCare post-procedure monitoring and clinic workflow tools.

Duration: for the term of the Clinic service relationship and any permitted retention period thereafter.

Nature and purpose of processing: hosting, transmitting, storing, and generating monitoring and workflow records as needed to provide SignalCare on Clinic instructions.

Types of personal information: patient identity and monitoring information (such as name, mobile, optional clinic identifier, enrolment and check-in content, SMS content, alerts, reviews, and clinical notes), plus staff account and clinic operational information.

Data subjects: Clinic patients enrolled or prepared for monitoring, and Clinic or organisation staff users.

Clinic instructions: use SignalCare features and APIs in the ordinary course, together with any documented support or deletion requests that SignalCare accepts.

SignalCare will not process patient information for advertising, brokerage, research products, cross-customer analytics products, or SignalCare model training on patient data.

## 9. Patient communications

The Clinic authorises SignalCare to send and receive patient communications through configured channels (currently SMS) for enrolled monitoring.

The Clinic is responsible for the clinical appropriateness of enrolments and for handling patient opt-outs and complaints raised to the Clinic.

SignalCare may include a short privacy reference or link in the first monitoring communication for an enrolment so patients can access the Patient Privacy and Monitoring Notice.

## 10. Subprocessors and service providers

SignalCare uses subprocessors and service providers to operate the service. Current providers include Twilio (SMS), Supabase (database and authentication), Amazon Web Services (API infrastructure and logs), Vercel (web application hosting), and Zoho (staff transactional email).

Primary production database and API infrastructure are in Australia. Some providers may process information outside SignalCare Australian infrastructure as part of delivering their services.

SignalCare will maintain a practical register of material subprocessors and will not make material changes that reduce overall privacy protections without reasonable notice where practicable.

The Clinic receives a non-exclusive, non-transferable right to use SignalCare during the subscription term for its internal clinical operations, subject to this Agreement and any Order Form.

The Clinic retains rights in its own patient and clinic content. The Clinic grants SignalCare a limited licence to host and process that content solely to provide and improve the operational service (without using patient data for SignalCare model training).

Patient personal information remains subject to the privacy terms above in addition to ordinary confidentiality duties.
